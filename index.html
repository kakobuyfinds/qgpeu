What to save on your shortlist

  1. Exact listing and variant. Save the product URL, color, size, price and date checked. Compare garment measurements with a piece you already own, or check the seller’s shoe sizing chart.
  2. Order eligibility and returns. Check whether HooBuy accepts the seller link and selected variant. Look up the exchange deadline and any domestic return charges before paying.
  3. Warehouse quality control. If photos are available, compare the ordered variant with the item label, front and back photos, and any requested measurement photos. Ask for a close-up if a detail is unclear.
  4. Parcel quote. Compare quotes for the same destination, packed dimensions and weight. Check which weight the route bills and whether taxes or handling fees are included.

Estimate your total cost

Add the item price, domestic delivery, international shipping and any fees or taxes you expect to pay. Convert each amount to USD before entering it.

Frequently asked questions

Can I order through HooBuy from a CAFANS listing?

Open the listing and check its agent options. If it provides an original seller URL, check that the URL and selected variant are accepted by HooBuy before paying.

Where can I check QC photos?

Look for warehouse inspection photos in your agent order if the service provides them. Compare the photos with the exact variant you ordered.

How do I compare two shipping quotes?

Use the same destination and package dimensions, then record the billable weight, included services, fees and estimated delivery window for each route.
